S-Divergence Parameter Surface 〈β, λ〉

Interactive 3D accuracy surface over the (β, λ) robustness parameter grid. Each point is the test accuracy of a ViT trained with SDIVLoss(β, λ) on medical imaging data. Hover for exact values.

ℓ(y,p) = (1/A)·Σₖ pₖ^{β+1} − (1+β)/(A·B)·pᵧ^B    where A = 1+λ(1−β), B = β−λ(1−β)
Data from: rSDNet experiments (Jana & Ghosh, arXiv:2603.17628) · ViT architecture: 4×4 patches, d=256, 6 layers, 8 heads, ~4M params · Seed=42 · 30 epochs
